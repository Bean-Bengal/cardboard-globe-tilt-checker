Tilt: Complement (wedge):

Cut the cradle wedge so the axle leans from vertical by the tilt. The complement is the angle between the axle and the table. Zones: tilt under 23.5 is below the tropics line, 23.5 up to but not over 66.5 is mid, over 66.5 is polar.
